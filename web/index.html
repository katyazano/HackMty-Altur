Architecture & Multi-Model Design Specifications

Comprehensive breakdown of both built-in models, why both exist in production, and how they complement each other.

E1

Engine 1: Baseline Fast CNN / GBM

Ultra-Low Latency

Designed for extreme high-throughput environments where sub-50ms CPU latency is required. Combines global spectral statistics with conversational turn-taking physics.

1. Spectral DSP & Timing (64-dim)

20 MFCCs + deltas, spectral contrast (4 bands), chroma, caller response latencies, speech overlap ratios.

2. 1D/2D AudioCNN (~250k Params)

Evaluates 4-second log-mel spectrogram patches with CMVN normalization to remove channel coloration.

3. Decision Meta-Learner

Weighted blend (50% HistGradientBoosting + 50% AudioCNN) with calibrated decision boundary.

Ideal for: Real-time edge streams, low CPU Avg Latency: ~30ms
E2

Engine 2: 4-Pillar Multimodal SOTA

SOTA Deep Defense

State-of-the-art multi-layer biometric anti-spoofing engine fusing Graph Neural Networks, raw waveforms, acoustic physics, and linguistic semantics into a 296-dimensional space.

Pillar 1: AASIST GNN (132-dim)

Graph Attention Networks uncover spectral-temporal artifact correlations across vocal formants.

Pillar 2: RawNet2 SincNet (4-dim)

Parameterized sinc convolutions learn directly from raw audio samples, avoiding Fourier phase loss.

Pillar 3: Acoustic DSP Physics (136-dim)

Wiener entropy, high-frequency energy ratio, pitch jitter, shimmer, and vocal tract formants.

Pillar 4: Whisper Multimodal & NLP (24-dim)

Whisper encoder attention latents + Mexican Spanish colloquial filler frequency and disfluency analysis.

Ideal for: High-risk fraud detection, zero-day TTS Avg Latency: ~180ms
