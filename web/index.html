Engine 1 Anatomy:
1. Global Acoustic & Spectral DSP (40-dim)

20 MFCCs, 20 first-order deltas, spectral contrast across 4 frequency sub-bands, chroma harmonic distribution, and zero-crossing rate.

2. Conversational Timing Physics (24-dim)

Dual-channel energy VAD isolates Caller (Ch 0) and Agent (Ch 1), computing turn-taking response latency CV, speech overlap ratios, and pause variances.

3. 1D/2D SpoofCNN Micro-Segments (~250k Params)

4-second log-mel CMVN normalized patches are scanned to detect localized vocoder phase artifacts that global averages dilate.
