Engine 1 Pipeline Architecture:
1. Global Spectral DSP (40-dim)

20 MFCCs, 20 first-order deltas, spectral contrast across 4 sub-bands, chroma harmonics, and zero-crossing rate.

2. Turn-Taking Timing Dynamics (24-dim)

Dual-channel energy VAD calculates conversational latency coefficient of variation, speech overlaps, and pause variance.

3. 1D/2D SpoofCNN (~250k Params)

Scans 4-second log-mel spectrogram patches with CMVN normalization to identify local vocoder phase anomalies.
